## Q2. What are DORA metrics?

Answer:

DORA metrics measure software delivery performance.

The four metrics are:

Deployment frequency:

- How often teams release changes.

Lead time for changes:

- Time from code commit to production.

Change failure rate:

- Percentage of deployments causing failures.

Mean time to recovery:

- Time to restore service after failure.

DORA metrics indicate engineering delivery maturity.

---

## Q3. How do DORA metrics improve DevEx?

Answer:

DORA metrics identify delivery bottlenecks.

Examples:

Low deployment frequency:

- Improve automation.

High lead time:

- Reduce process friction.

High failure rate:

- Improve testing.

Slow recovery:

- Improve observability.

The goal is improving engineering systems, not increasing pressure on teams.

## Q5. Why should organizations avoid measuring developers only by activity?

Answer:

Activity metrics can create harmful incentives.

Examples:

Poor metrics:

- Number of commits.
- Lines of code.
- Hours worked.

Problems:

- Encourage quantity over quality.
- Ignore collaboration.
- Misrepresent productivity.

Better measures:

- Delivery outcomes.
- Quality.
- Developer experience.
- Business impact.

Good metrics improve systems rather than judge individuals.
